PopLauncher Privacy Policy

Intentional Friction, Minimalist Design & Privacy-First Android Launcher
Last Updated: September 26, 2026

🛡️ Privacy Summary at a Glance

PopLauncher is built from the ground up on the principle of Zero Data Harvesting. We do NOT collect, sell, lease, or track your personal information. App searches and friction settings execute 100% on-device. No telemetry trackers, no targeted advertising SDKs, and no hidden profiling.

1. Overview & Philosophy

PopLauncher is an Android Home Screen (Launcher) application designed to promote mindful smartphone usage through intentional friction (such as requiring full app name search and popping floating physics bubbles) and customizable home widgets. Because PopLauncher is an essential utility operating directly on your device, we treat privacy and security as non-negotiable core requirements.

2. Data Collection & Usage

A. Installed Applications (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES)

As a core launcher application, PopLauncher requires access to query the list of installed applications on your device. This data is processed strictly on-device in volatile memory to:

  • Display your applications in the home screen, app drawer, and search interface.
  • Enable intentional friction controls for apps you designate (e.g., social media).
  • Render application icons and shortcuts on the launcher canvas.

Your installed application list is never transmitted to our servers, third parties, or cloud storage.

B. On-Device Contact Search (READ_CONTACTS)

If you optionally enable contact search in PopLauncher, the launcher accesses your device's local address book solely while you type in the search bar. This enables you to quickly initiate phone calls, WhatsApp messages, or SMS threads directly from the search bar.

🔒 Google Play Personal & Sensitive User Data Policy:

Contact information is never uploaded, synced to any external server, stored in external databases, or shared with third parties. It is queried strictly on-device using Android's native ContentResolver and discarded immediately upon closing the search overlay. Contact access is completely optional and can be revoked at any time in system settings without breaking launcher functionality.

C. Custom AI Widgets & Gemini Pro Integration

PopLauncher features an AI Playground where you can create custom, interactive widgets (e.g., hydration counters, habit trackers, retro digital clocks) through conversational prompts:

  • Self-Hosted / Laptop Server Routing: When using custom AI widget generation, requests containing only your widget prompt and widget structure are routed to your designated personal server / laptop running your Gemini Pro endpoint, or directly to Google Gemini's API if you provide your personal API key.
  • No Personal Identifiers: Widget prompts do not include personal identity tokens, device hardware IDs, contact records, or phone numbers.
  • Local Widget Storage: All generated widgets and their persistent interactive states (e.g., step counters, habit tallies) are stored exclusively in private on-device SharedPreferences.

D. Launcher Preferences & Friction Configuration

Settings such as bubble physics speed, friction application selections, accent color themes, and widget layouts are stored exclusively in private Android SharedPreferences on your local hardware.

3. Android Permissions Justification

Below is the full declaration of permissions declared in PopLauncher and their compliance with Google Play Developer Programme Policies:

Permission Purpose & Google Play Compliance Justification
QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES Permitted for Home/Launcher apps (CATEGORY_HOME). Essential to index, categorize, and launch installed user apps.
BIND_APPWIDGET Allows hosting Android AppWidgets directly on PopLauncher's dedicated widget canvas.
READ_CONTACTS Optional runtime permission for local, on-device contact search and quick actions. Never uploaded or shared.
INTERNET Used for optional Google Search overviews and communicating with the user's self-hosted Gemini Pro server for custom widget generation.
VIBRATE Provides tactile haptic feedback when popping floating bubbles and interacting with custom widget controls.

4. U.S. Export Administration Regulations (EAR) Compliance

PopLauncher complies with United States Export Administration Regulations (EAR):

U.S. EAR Export Classification:

PopLauncher utilizes standard industry encryption solely for HTTPS/TLS communication (utilizing Android's built-in cryptographic libraries and Java Secure Socket Layer). It qualifies under EAR99 or ECCN 5D992.c / Mass Market exemption under 15 CFR § 740.17(b)(1). No proprietary or non-standard cryptographic systems are incorporated.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

PopLauncher does not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13. PopLauncher does not contain age-inappropriate content, behavioral advertising, or tracking technologies.

6. Third-Party Services & Advertising

PopLauncher contains zero third-party advertising networks and zero analytics/tracking SDKs (such as Facebook SDK, AppsFlyer, or Firebase Analytics). When conducting web searches, queries are dispatched directly to the public search engine or AI overview provider of your choice without tracking cookies.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all launcher preferences and custom widgets are stored locally in the application's sandboxed storage:

  • Clearing the app storage in Android System Settings completely and irreversibly deletes all stored configurations.
  • Uninstalling PopLauncher immediately erases all associated data from your device.
